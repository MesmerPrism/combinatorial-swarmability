`MesmerPrism/rusty-matter` at exact revision
`54a18d85ee877f5cb6c8275013da8a76764b140e`. Those crates and their transitive

is not committed or installed globally.

JavaScript, prose, and synthetic data. No website font binaries, private
research attachments, participant data, or third-party paper assets are copied
into this repository.
