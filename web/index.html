    What this atlas preserves

    Input is not authority

    Pointer, keyboard, switch-style activation, gesture, pose, MIDI, or replay can normalize into the same semantic request. Scope and authority remain explicit policy rather than device side effects.

    One deterministic core

    The Rust reducer resolves scope and executes local alignment, cohesion, separation, and per-member rules before producing Rusty Matter payloads. Browser controls and Canvas2D remain adapters.

    Evidence is not reproduction

    The versioned public catalogue keeps source reports, evidence status, transfer limits, and app reconstruction claims visibly separate.

    Deterministic public demo. No accounts, analytics, cookies, or behavioral logging.
